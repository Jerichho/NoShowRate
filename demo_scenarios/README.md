# Demo Scenarios for Airline No-Show Prediction

Use these scenarios to demonstrate the system:

## Scenario 1: High-Value Business Traveler
**Description**: Premium business traveler with short lead time

**Input Parameters**:
- Booking Date: 2025-09-22
- Flight Date: 2025-09-24
- Route: NYC-LON
- Fare Class: Business
- Customer Type: Business
- Lead Time: 2 days
- Expected Risk: Low

**Demo Talking Points**:
- This represents a reliable business traveler
- Short lead time but premium fare class
- Expected no-show probability: 10-20%
